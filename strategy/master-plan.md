## The Math That Matters

- **Prelims GS Paper I**: 100 questions × 2 marks = 200 marks (–0.66 per wrong)
- **Prelims CSAT Paper II**: 80 questions × 2.5 marks = 200 marks (–0.83 per wrong)
  - **Qualifying**: 33% = 66 marks ≈ 27 correct with 0 wrong, or ~30 correct with a few wrong
- **Typical Cutoff (General)**: ~90-100/200 in GS (varies by year)
- **OBC Cutoff**: Usually 5-10 marks lower than General

### Your Target
- **GS Paper I**: 110+ marks (≈55+ correct, <10 wrong, rest unattempted)
- **CSAT**: 80+ marks (safely above 66 qualifying cutoff)

## The 3 Non-Negotiable Daily Habits

1. **10 PYQ questions daily** — with elimination technique, timed (15 min max)
2. **15 min current affairs** — UPSC-filtered, not newspaper browsing
3. **5 min daily log** — what you studied, what you got wrong, mood check
