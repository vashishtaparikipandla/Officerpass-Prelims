## 2. Daily Time Architecture (3–4 Hours / Day)

To survive a demanding 10–11 hour job without burnout, consistency beats marathon hours. As Neil Sir (HCS #93) states: *"Consistency over everything. Same effort spread cleanly."*

### Morning Block (2 Hours: 06:00 – 08:00 AM) — Deep Cognitive Core
*Your mind is freshest before office emails and calls begin.*
- **06:00 – 07:30 (90 min)**: **High-Yield Static Pillar** (Polity / Economy / Geography / Environment). Deep conceptual reading on iPad.
- **07:30 – 08:00 (30 min)**: **CSAT Daily Drill** (2 Reading Comprehension passages + 3 basic numeracy/reasoning problems). Daily micro-dosing makes CSAT effortless by exam day.

### Evening Block (1.5 – 2 Hours: 08:30 – 10:30 PM) — Active Application & Consolidation
*After office when passive reading leads to sleep, we do active problem-solving.*
- **08:30 – 09:15 (45 min)**: **PYQ Elimination Drill** (10–12 questions from the UnlockIAS compilations). Strict rule: *Mark initial instinct immediately. No second-guessing.*
- **09:15 – 09:45 (30 min)**: **UPSC Current Affairs Digest** (Theme-based, static-linked. No newspaper drama).
- **09:45 – 10:15 (30 min)**: Daily Log entry + Rapid Flashcard / Formula / Article revision.

### Weekend Rule (Saturday & Sunday)
- **Same 3–4 hours duration** (to prevent weekend burnout and keep sleep cycles steady).
- Format changes: 1 timed 50-Q sectional test or CSAT sectional test + in-depth wrong-answer post-mortem.

## 3. Candidate Core Rules

1. **OMR Rule**: When answering practice questions, mark the answer immediately. Do not leave it floating for a second pass.
2. **Current Affairs Rule**: No 2-hour daily newspaper reading. Rely on theme-based monthly digests and static linkages.
3. **CSAT Rule**: Never skip CSAT. 30 minutes daily practice is non-negotiable.
4. **Honesty Rule**: Daily log must record every question where an urge to change was felt.
