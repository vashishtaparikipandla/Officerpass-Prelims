## 3. The 5 Classic UPSC Geography Traps
1. **The Tributary Bank Trap (Left vs Right Bank)**:
   - UPSC repeatedly asks tributary combinations. E.g., Yamuna tributaries (Chambal, Betwa, Ken are right bank; Hindon is left bank).
   - Godavari tributaries: Pranhita, Indravati, Sabari (left bank); Manjira (right bank).
2. **The River Origin & Drainage Destination Trap**:
   - West-flowing rivers (Narmada, Tapi, Sabarmati, Mahi, Mandovi, Zuari, Periyar) vs East-flowing rivers into Bay of Bengal.
   - Origin of Indus/Sutlej/Brahmaputra in Tibet near Lake Manasarovar (antecedent drainage).
3. **The Latitude / Daylight Variation Trap**:
   - Daylight hours in summer/winter based on distance from equator.
   - Longitude alignment: Which cities lie on nearly the same longitude (e.g., Delhi and Bengaluru).
4. **The Strategic Sea Bordering Countries Trap**:
   - Countries bordering Mediterranean (TRICK: "RIP CLUES" or similar mnemonics).
   - Countries bordering Black Sea (BURGER: Bulgaria, Ukraine, Russia, Georgia, Romania + Turkey).
   - Countries bordering Caspian Sea (TARIK: Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan, Russia, Iran, Kazakhstan).
   - Red Sea (DESSEI: Djibouti, Eritrea, Saudi Arabia, Sudan, Egypt, Yemen).
5. **Crop Conditions Trap**:
   - Rainfall and temperature requirements for Tea, Coffee, Sugarcane, Cotton, Rice, Wheat, Pulses. E.g., Coffee requires well-drained loamy soil and shade, frost is fatal.

## 4. 10 High-Yield Geography Checkpoints
1. **Indian Drainage Mastery**: Complete tributary mapping of Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra, Narmada, Tapi, Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery.
2. **Monsoon Dynamic Factors**: Tibetan Plateau heating, Somali Jet, Tropical Easterly Jet, Mascarene High, Indian Ocean Dipole (Positive vs Negative).
3. **Soil Profiles of India**: Black Cotton Soil (self-ploughing, moisture retentive), Laterite Soil (leached, iron & aluminium rich, acidic), Alluvial Soil (potash rich, phosphorus deficient).
4. **Major Mountain Passes**: Shipki La, Nathu La, Zoji La, Lipulekh, Rohtang, Palghat, Bhorghat, Thalghat.
5. **Major Ports & Maritime Infrastructure**: 12 Major Ports of India, location, natural vs artificial, landlord port model.
6. **Critical Straits & Chokepoints**: Strait of Malacca, Bab-el-Mandeb, Strait of Hormuz, Bosphorus & Dardanelles, Gibraltar.
7. **Major Minerals in India**: Gondwana Coal distribution (Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh), Iron ore (Bailadila, Kudremukh, Mayurbhanj), Bauxite (Kalahandi, Koraput).
8. **World Deserts & Climate Types**: Tropical hot deserts (location on western margins due to cold currents and offshore trade winds).
9. **Ocean Currents**: Benguela, Humboldt (Peru), Canaries, Kuroshio, Oyashio, Gulf Stream — and their climatic/fisheries impacts.
10. **Regions Frequently in News**: Sahel, Horn of Africa, Levant, Donbas, Nagorno-Karabakh, Gaza, Senkaku/Diaoyu Islands, Spratly Islands.
