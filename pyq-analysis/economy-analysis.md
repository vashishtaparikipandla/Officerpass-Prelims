## 3. The 5 Classic UPSC Economy Traps
1. **The Inverse Monetary Mechanism Trap**:
   - UPSC tests if you mix up causation: *\"An increase in the Repo Rate by RBI leads to an increase in money supply in the economy.\"* (False — it tightens credit, reduces money supply).
2. **CPI vs WPI Comparison Trap**:
   - *Headline inflation in India uses WPI.* (False — shifted to CPI-Combined since 2014 based on Urjit Patel Committee).
   - *Services are included in WPI.* (False — goods only).
   - *Food weight is higher in CPI than WPI.* (True — ~45.86% in CPI vs ~24.38% in WPI).
3. **FDI vs FPI Boundary Trap**:
   - Portfolio investment (FPI) is passive, liquid, short-term, generally <10% holding.
   - Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) is active management, non-debt creating, long-term, >=10% stake.
4. **Current Account vs Capital Account Classification**:
   - Remittances, gifts, grants = Invisible receipts in *Current Account*.
   - External Commercial Borrowings (ECB), Foreign loans = *Capital Account*.
5. **Money Multiplier Fallacy**:
   - UPSC asked: *\"Money multiplier in an economy increases with which of the following?\"*
   - Trap: Lowering CRR increases it, but increasing banking habit of the population directly expands it.

## 4. 10 High-Yield Economy Hotspots to Master
1. **Monetary Policy Framework**: RBI MPC composition (6 members, 4-year term, Governor has casting vote), Policy tools (Repo, Reverse Repo, SDF, MSF, OMO).
2. **Inflation Dynamics**: CPI-C vs WPI differences, Headline vs Core inflation, Base effect, Phillips curve.
3. **Government Budgeting**: Capital Expenditure vs Revenue Expenditure; Revenue Deficit, Effective Revenue Deficit, Fiscal Deficit, Primary Deficit.
4. **External Sector Concepts**: Balance of Payments components, Convertibility (Current account full vs Capital account partial), NEER/REER, Currency appreciation/depreciation effects on imports/exports.
5. **Agricultural Economics**: Minimum Support Price (MSP) — crops covered (22 mandated + FRP for sugarcane), CACP recommendations, Fertilizer subsidies (Urea vs Nutrient Based Subsidy).
6. **Banking Terminology**: Basel III capital ratios (CAR/CRAR), Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) framework, Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC).
7. **Debt & Bond Markets**: Bond yield vs Bond price inverse relationship, Yield inversion, Sovereign Green Bonds, Treasury Bills (91, 182, 364 days).
8. **National Income Accounting**: GDP deflator, Gross Value Added (GVA), Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) ranking.
9. **International Financial Architecture**: IMF quota & SDR basket currencies (USD, EUR, CNY, JPY, GBP), World Bank vs IMF mandates.
10. **Trade Mechanisms**: Most Favoured Nation (MFN) principle, Generalized System of Preferences (GSP), Free Trade Agreements (FTAs), Anti-dumping vs Countervailing duties.
