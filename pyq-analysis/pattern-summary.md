## 2. The 'Four Pillars' Strategy (70% of the Exam)

The data proves that UPSC Prelims is heavily anchored on 4 core subjects:
1. **Economy** (~17 Qs)
2. **Environment** (~16 Qs)
3. **Polity** (~14 Qs)
4. **Geography** (~12 Qs)

**Together, these 4 subjects constitute 59 to 65 questions out of 100 every single year.**
- Mastering the 4 Pillars gives you direct access to **118 – 130 marks**.
- History (all 4 parts combined) accounts for only ~15 questions.
- Science & Tech + Current Affairs account for ~25 questions.
