## 3. The 5 Classic UPSC Polity Traps
1. **The "Provided in the Constitution" Trap**:
   - UPSC writes: *"The Constitution of India defines..."* or *"As per the Constitution of India..."*
   - Fact: Words like *"Cabinet"* (except Art 352), *"Office of Profit"*, *"Basic Structure"*, *"Contempt of Court"* are NOT defined in the Constitution!
2. **The Ministry / Authority Switch**:
   - Assigning a statutory body to the wrong ministry (e.g., CBI under Ministry of Home Affairs instead of Personnel, CEIB under Home instead of Finance).
3. **The Mandatory vs Discretionary Trap**:
   - Changing *"may"* to *"shall"* or *"is obligated to"*. E.g., Governor's assent or President's consultation.
4. **The "Sole Power" Trap**:
   - Claiming only Lok Sabha or only Rajya Sabha has a certain power when both share it (or vice-versa, e.g., All India Services Art 312 is RS initiation only).
5. **The 10th Schedule (Anti-Defection) Fine Print**:
   - Nominated members joining after 6 months vs Independent members joining anytime.

## 4. 10 Must-Master Core Chapters in Laxmikanth
1. **Preamble & Salient Features** (Constitutionalism, Sovereign/Socialist/Secular/Democratic/Republic meanings)
2. **Fundamental Rights (Art 12–35)** & Writs (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo-Warranto)
3. **DPSP (Art 36–51)** & Fundamental Duties (Art 51A)
4. **President & Governor Comparative Powers** (Pardoning power Art 72 vs 161, Ordinance Art 123 vs 213, Discretion)
5. **Parliament: Procedures, Motions & Financial Bills** (Money Bill Art 110, Financial Bills I & II, Cut motions, Adjournment)
6. **Parliamentary Committees** (Public Accounts Committee, Estimates Committee, Committee on Public Undertakings)
7. **Supreme Court & High Courts** (Original, Appellate, Writ, Advisory jurisdiction, NJAC/Collegium, Contempt)
8. **73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments** (Compulsory vs Voluntary provisions, State Election Commission, State Finance Commission)
9. **Emergency Provisions** (National Art 352, President's Rule Art 356, Financial Art 360 - revocation, effects on FRs)
10. **Constitutional vs Non-Constitutional Bodies** (Election Commission, CAG, Finance Commission vs NITI Aayog, NHRC, CIC)
