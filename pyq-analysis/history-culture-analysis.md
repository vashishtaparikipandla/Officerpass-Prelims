# History & Art-Culture — PYQ Deep Analysis (1995–2026)
> Based on **734 Combined Questions** (Ancient: 101, Medieval: 92, Modern: 321, Art & Culture: 220) from official UPSC papers.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Weightage Trend
- **The Shift**: In the early 2000s, Modern History was 15–20 questions. Today, Modern History has shrunk to **4–7 questions**, while **Ancient History, Medieval terms, and Art & Culture** have expanded.
- **Combined Annual Weightage**: ~14 to 17 questions across all 4 segments:
  - Modern History: ~4 to 7 Qs
  - Art & Culture: ~4 to 6 Qs
  - Ancient History: ~3 to 6 Qs
  - Medieval History: ~1 to 4 Qs
- **Strategy for Working Professionals**: **Do NOT get bogged down in massive history volumes.** Focus strictly on high-yield recurring themes (Buddhism/Jainism, Mauryan/Gupta architecture, Bhakti/Sufi saints, Vijayanagara Empire, Gandhian movements 1915-1942, and Constitutional Acts 1909/1919/1935).

---

## 2. Subject-Wise High-Yield Subtopics

### A. Ancient History (101 Questions)
1. **Buddhism & Jainism** (Core philosophy: Bodhisattvas, Hinayana vs Mahayana vs Vajrayana, Councils, Jain Tirthankaras, Syadvada, Anekantavada).
2. **Mauryan Empire** (Ashokan Edicts: Major/Minor Rock Edicts, locations, Dhamma, Arthashastra administrative terms).
3. **Indus Valley Civilization** (Dholavira water reservoir, Lothal dockyard, Rakhigarhi, absence of horse/iron evidence).
4. **Gupta & Post-Gupta Era** (Literature: Kalidasa, Vishakhadatta; Science: Aryabhata, Varahamihira; Feudal terms: Vishti).
5. **Vedic Period & Sangam Literature** (Tolkoppiyam, Silappadikaram, Manimekalai, 5 eco-zones / Thinai).

### B. Medieval History (92 Questions)
1. **Vijayanagara Empire** (Travellers: Nicolo Conti, Abdur Razzaq, Domingo Paes, Fernao Nuniz; Rayas, Nayakas, Amara-nayaka system).
2. **Delhi Sultanate** (Administration: Iqtadari system, Market regulations of Alauddin Khalji, Token currency of MBT).
3. **Mughal Empire** (Mansabdari & Jagirdari system, Zabti system, Todar Mal's Bandobast, Din-i Ilahi, Architecture).
4. **Bhakti & Sufi Movements** (Kabir, Guru Nanak, Mirabai, Shankaradeva, Ramanuja, Chaitanya; Chisti & Suhrawardi silsilas).
5. **Medieval Revenue Terms** (Ghari, Charai, Jizya, Chauth & Sardeshmukhi of Marathas).

### C. Modern History (321 Questions)
1. **Gandhian Mass Movements (1915–1942)**:
   - Non-Cooperation Movement (1920–22), Civil Disobedience Movement & Salt Satyagraha (1930), Quit India Movement (1942).
2. **Constitutional Developments (1909–1947)**:
   - Morley-Minto (1909: Separate Electorates), Montagu-Chelmsford (1919: Dyarchy in Provinces), GoI Act 1935 (Provincial Autonomy, Federal Court).
   - August Offer (1940), Cripps Mission (1942), Wavell Plan (1945), Cabinet Mission (1946).
3. **Socio-Religious Reform Movements**:
   - Brahmo Samaj (Raja Ram Mohan Roy), Arya Samaj (Dayanand Saraswati), Satyashodhak Samaj (Jyotirao Phule), Young Bengal, Servants of India Society.
4. **Early Nationalist Era (1885–1915)**:
   - Moderates vs Extremists, Surat Split (1907), Swadeshi Movement (1905), Lucknow Pact (1916).
5. **Tribal & Peasant Uprisings**:
   - Santhal Hool (1855), Munda Ulgulan (Birsa Munda), Indigo Revolt (1859), Deccan Riots, Moplah Rebellion.

### D. Art & Culture (220 Questions)
1. **Temple Architecture & Sculptures**:
   - Nagara vs Dravida vs Vesara styles, Panchayatan style, Chola bronzes (Nataraja), Pallava rock-cut temples, Ellora/Ajanta caves.
2. **Indian Philosophy**:
   - 6 Orthodox Schools (Shad-Darshana: Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, Vedanta) + Heterodox (Charvaka, Ajivika, Jain, Buddhist).
3. **Classical Dance & Music Forms**:
   - 8 Classical dances (Sangeet Natak Akademi), Hindustani vs Carnatic music features.
4. **UNESCO World Heritage Sites & Intangible Heritage**:
   - Recent inscriptions, architectural peculiarities.
