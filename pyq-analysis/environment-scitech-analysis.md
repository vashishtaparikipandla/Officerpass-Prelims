## 4. The 5 Classic Traps in Environment & SciTech
1. **The \"Can be completely eradicated\" Trap**:
   - In S&T, absolute assertions about eliminating diseases or genetic risks are almost always FALSE.
2. **The WPA 1972 Schedule Confusion**:
   - The Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022 rationalized schedules from 6 down to 4:
     - Schedule I: Highest level of protection for animal species.
     - Schedule II: Animal species with lesser protection.
     - Schedule III: Protected plant species.
     - Schedule IV: Specimens listed under CITES.
     - Vermin schedule eliminated!
3. **The National Park vs Wildlife Sanctuary Rights Trap**:
   - National Parks: Greater degree of protection; no human activity/grazing allowed without Chief Wildlife Warden approval.
   - Sanctuaries: Regulated human activities and traditional rights may be permitted.
4. **The Treaty Governance Trap**:
   - Mixing up Secretariats: CITES is administered by UNEP; Ramsar is partnered with IUCN (not UNEP); CMS is UNEP.
5. **The Emerging Tech Exaggeration Trap**:
   - UPSC tests futuristic capabilities vs present limitations. E.g., CRISPR editing in somatic cells is NOT inheritable; only germline editing is passed to offspring.
