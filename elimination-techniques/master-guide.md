# Elimination Techniques for UPSC Prelims

> **Core Principle**: In UPSC, you don't need to know the answer.  
> You need to eliminate the wrong ones.  
> Knowing 60% is enough to score 90% — IF you eliminate well.

---

## Technique 1: The Extreme Word Trap

**Rule**: UPSC loves nuance. Options with extreme/absolute words are usually wrong.

**Red Flag Words** (usually wrong):
- Always, Never, Only, All, None, Completely, Entirely
- "is the sole reason", "is the only factor"
- "was first established by", "is exclusively found in"

**Safe Words** (more likely correct):
- Generally, Often, Usually, May, Can, Sometimes
- "is one of the factors", "is primarily associated with"

**Practice**: Before marking, scan your chosen answer for extreme words. If it has one, re-examine.

---

## Technique 2: The Odd One Out

**Rule**: In a list-matching or "which of the following" question, one option usually doesn't fit the category at all.

**Method**:
1. Read all 4 options before thinking about the answer
2. Find the one that is clearly from a different domain/category
3. Eliminate it immediately
4. Now you have a 1-in-3 chance minimum

---

## Technique 3: The 2-2 Split Decision

**Rule**: When you've eliminated 2 options and are stuck between the remaining 2.

**Protocol**:
1. ⏱️ Spend MAX 30 seconds on the split
2. Go with your **first instinct** — research shows first instinct is correct 65-70% of the time
3. Mark it and **DO NOT RETURN** to this question
4. If you genuinely have zero instinct, **leave it blank** (negative marking protection)

> [!CAUTION]
> **THIS IS YOUR BIGGEST ENEMY**: Changing answers after a 2-2 split.  
> Your previous attempts show you overthink at this exact moment.  
> The protocol is: FIRST INSTINCT → MARK → MOVE → NEVER RETURN.

---

## Technique 4: The Statement Combination Shortcut

**For questions like**: "Which of the above statements is/are correct?"
- Options: (a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) 1 and 3 (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Method**:
1. Find the ONE statement you're most confident about (right or wrong)
2. Use that to eliminate options
3. Example: If you're sure Statement 2 is WRONG → eliminate (b) and (d)
4. Now you only need to figure out Statement 3 to choose between (a) and (c)

**Power Move**: You only need to be confident about 1 statement to eliminate 2 options.

---

## Technique 5: The Current Affairs Anchor

**Rule**: If a question's topic was in the news in the last 18 months, the "newsworthy" angle is usually part of the answer.

**Method**:
1. Recognize if the topic was recently in news
2. The answer usually aligns with WHY it was in the news
3. Use news context to validate or eliminate options

---

## Technique 6: The Constitutional Logic Check

**For Polity questions**:
- If an option gives Parliament MORE power than it logically should → likely correct (Parliament is supreme in Indian framework)
- If an option gives State something that should be Union → likely wrong
- If an option contradicts basic federal structure → usually wrong
- Apply "basic structure doctrine" as a logical filter

---

## Technique 7: The Geographical Reasonability Test

**For Geography questions**:
- Apply basic climate/latitude/monsoon logic
- "Does this make sense for this location?"
- Example: "Tropical evergreen forests in Rajasthan" → Immediately eliminate
- Use physical geography basics to filter factual options

---

## Technique 8: The Time Budget Protocol

**GS Paper I: 2 hours for 100 questions**

| Phase | Time | Questions | Purpose |
|-------|------|-----------|---------|
| First Pass | 70 min | All 100 | Answer confident ones, mark unsure, skip unknown |
| Second Pass | 35 min | Marked ones | Apply elimination on 2-2 splits |
| Final Check | 15 min | Review marked answers | ONLY check for bubbling errors, NOT content |

**Rules**:
- **First pass**: If you can't decide in 60 seconds → MARK and MOVE
- **Second pass**: 2-2 splits get 30 seconds max, then first instinct or leave blank
- **Final check**: DO NOT change any answers. Only verify bubble sheet accuracy.

---

## Technique 9: The Negative Marking Calculator

**When to attempt vs. skip**:
- **Attempt if**: You can eliminate 2 or more options (50%+ chance = positive expected value)
- **Leave blank if**: You cannot eliminate even 1 option (pure guess = negative expected value)
- **Math**: 
  - Right = +2.0, Wrong = -0.66
  - At 50% chance (2 options eliminated): Expected = 0.5(2) + 0.5(-0.66) = +0.67 → ATTEMPT
  - At 33% chance (1 option eliminated): Expected = 0.33(2) + 0.67(-0.66) = +0.22 → ATTEMPT (marginal)
  - At 25% chance (0 eliminated): Expected = 0.25(2) + 0.75(-0.66) = +0.005 → SKIP

> [!TIP]
> **Bottom line**: If you can eliminate even 1 option, attempt the question.
> If you're doing pure random guessing across 4 options, skip.

## Technique 10: The Anti-Overthinking Protocol

**Your Personal Rule (NON-NEGOTIABLE)**:

```
IF first_instinct exists AND time_on_question > 60 seconds:
    MARK first_instinct
    MOVE to next question
    DO NOT RETURN

IF changed_mind_after_marking:
    IGNORE the new thought
    KEEP original answer
    This is your overthinking brain — it is WRONG more often than your instinct
```

**The Science**: Studies on MCQ test-taking consistently show:
- First instinct is correct ~65% of the time
- Changed answers go from right→wrong more often than wrong→right
- The more you think about an uncertain answer, the worse your decision gets
- Your brain manufactures false logic to justify changing answers

**Training Method**: In every practice test, mark questions where you wanted to change your answer. Track whether the change would have helped or hurt. Build data proving your first instinct is better.

---

## Daily Elimination Drill

Every day, do 10 PYQs with this strict protocol:
1. Set a timer: 10 minutes for 10 questions
2. For each question, write down:
   - Which options you eliminated and WHY
   - Which technique you used
   - Your first instinct
   - Your final answer
   - Whether you wanted to change it
3. After checking answers, analyze:
   - How many eliminations were correct?
   - Did first instinct beat changed answers?
   - Which techniques worked best?

This builds the elimination muscle. After 30 days of this, you'll eliminate automatically.
