## The CSAT Game Plan

### Priority Order (by ROI — marks per hour of prep)

#### 1. Reading Comprehension (HIGHEST ROI)
- Contributes 30-35% of the paper
- Most improvable with technique
- **Zero knowledge required** — answer is IN the passage

**Technique**:
1. Read the QUESTIONS first (not the passage)
2. Scan the passage for specific answer areas
3. Answer directly from passage text — don't infer beyond what's written
4. UPSC RC passages have one and only one correct interpretation
5. If the answer isn't directly stated/implied in the passage, it's wrong

**Daily Practice**: 3 RC passages/day (15 minutes total)

#### 2. Logical Reasoning
- Patterns, sequences, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense
- Formulaic — once you learn the method, it's mechanical

**Technique**:
- Learn each sub-type method (not theory, just method)
- Practice 5 questions/day for each sub-type until automatic
- Time yourself — if method takes >90 seconds, you're doing it wrong

**Weekly Practice**: 1 sub-type per week, 5 questions/day

#### 3. Basic Numeracy
- Percentages, ratios, averages, profit/loss, time-speed-distance
- Class 8-10 level math — but tricky under time pressure

**Technique**:
- Learn shortcuts (not textbook methods)
- Approximation > exact calculation for UPSC
- Example: If answer options are 23%, 37%, 52%, 71% — rough mental math is enough
- Master percentage↔fraction shortcuts: 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/6 = 16.67%, etc.

**Practice**: 5 numeracy questions/day

#### 4. Analytical Ability
- Data interpretation, logical deduction from given data
- Read tables/graphs carefully — answer is in the data

#### 5. Decision Making
- Least important section, least frequent
- Apply "most ethical + most practical" rule and move on
