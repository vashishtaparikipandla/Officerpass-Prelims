### 🧠 The 3 Memory Anchors for Prelims
1. **Who can summon a Joint Sitting?** The **President** (Article 108).
2. **Who presides?** The **Speaker of Lok Sabha** (or Deputy Speaker, or Deputy Chairman of RS — *the Chairman of RS / Vice President NEVER presides!*).
3. **Where is a Joint Sitting FORBIDDEN?** Money Bills (Art 110) and Constitutional Amendment Bills (Art 368).

                 DEMAND COOLS OFF → INFLATION FALLS! 🎯

### The Bond Market Seesaw (UPSC Favorite Concept)
```
          HIGH INTEREST RATES
                  ▲
                 / \
                /   \
               /     \
              /       ▼
                       BOND PRICES CRASH
```
*Why?* If new government bonds give 8% interest, nobody wants to buy your old bond that pays only 6%. To sell your old bond, you must drop its price!  
**Mnemonic**: *Interest Rate goes UP $\longleftrightarrow$ Bond Price goes DOWN.*

## Story 5: CSAT Mental Model — The "₹100 Note" in Your Pocket

Whenever a UPSC question talks about percentage increases, price rises, or expenditure adjustments: **Never write algebraic $x$. Reach into your mental pocket and pull out a crisp ₹100 note.**

```
Step 1: Original Apple = ₹100
Step 2: Price inflates by 25% → New Apple = ₹125
Step 3: But your pocket only holds ₹100!
Step 4: You cannot buy the full apple. How much do you give back?
        ₹25 out of the new price of ₹125:
                 25 / 125 = 1/5 = 20%!
```
*Visual Ladder*:
$$\frac{1}{2}\ (50\%) \longleftrightarrow \frac{1}{3}\ (33\%) \longleftrightarrow \frac{1}{4}\ (25\%) \longleftrightarrow \frac{1}{5}\ (20\%) \longleftrightarrow \frac{1}{6}\ (16.7\%)$$
- If price climbs by $\frac{1}{4}$ ($25\%$), consumption drops by the neighboring smaller rung: $\frac{1}{5}$ ($20\%$).
- If price climbs by $\frac{1}{3}$ ($33\%$), consumption drops by $\frac{1}{4}$ ($25\%$).
